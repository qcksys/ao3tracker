Update downloaded works in the background and show clearer download status.

Downloaded works now fetch new and revised chapters automatically when sync reports an update; turn this off under Settings → Offline reading. The Works screen shows an overall download status beside Downloads, and the download notification shows the chapter currently being saved or updated. Tapping the notification opens a new Download queue section at the top of Downloads. A work's offline reading section starts collapsed to its status, and queued downloads offer Open on AO3 to reconnect.
